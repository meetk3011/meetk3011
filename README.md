## Hi there 👋


I am an environmental researcher and geospatial analyst with experience in **GIS, remote sensing, hydrology, environmental monitoring, spatial analysis, conservation science, and scientific communication**.

I recently completed an **Honours Bachelor of Environmental Studies in Environmental Management** and a **Certificate in Geomatics: GIS and Remote Sensing** at York University, graduating **summa cum laude**. I am now beginning graduate studies at the **University of Waterloo**, where my research focuses on remote sensing and geospatial analysis for agricultural and hydrological applications.

---

## 🔭 Current work

I currently work as a **Hydrometeorology Research Assistant** with the University of Waterloo’s Hydrometeorology Research Group.

My current work includes:

- Developing Python and Google Earth Engine workflows for preprocessing, compositing, and analyzing multisource remote-sensing data
- Producing long-term Sentinel-1 soil-moisture datasets for agricultural research sites
- Processing Sentinel-2 imagery for vegetation indices and crop phenology
- Generating high-resolution digital terrain models and terrain derivatives
- Analyzing slope, aspect, flow accumulation, and topographic wetness indices
- Exploring evapotranspiration modelling and the transfer of OpenET-based methods to Canadian agricultural fields
- Conducting geospatial time-series analysis, model validation, and visualization
